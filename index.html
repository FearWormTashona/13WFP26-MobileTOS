Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

13th World Fright Park ("13th World Fright Park," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website, purchase tickets, sign up for email or SMS communications, participate in promotions, or otherwise interact with us.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • Billing and transaction information
  • Ticket purchase and event information
  • Marketing and communication preferences
  • Information submitted through forms, contests, promotions, or surveys
  • Other information you choose to provide to us

We may also automatically collect certain information when you use our website, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, referring pages, and information collected through cookies and similar technologies.

How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Process ticket purchases and other transactions
  • Provide information about your purchases or event attendance
  • Respond to questions and customer service requests
  • Send email and SMS marketing communications when you have opted in
  • Provide promotional offers, event announcements, and updates
  • Administer contests, promotions, surveys, and other features
  • Improve our website, events, marketing, and customer experience
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, and security threats
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations

Email Communications

If you subscribe to our email marketing communications, we may send you information about 13th World Fright Park, including event announcements, ticket offers, promotions, and other marketing messages.

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time by using the unsubscribe link included in our emails. You may still receive transactional or service-related communications when necessary.

SMS Privacy

If you opt in to receive SMS messages from 13th World Fright Park, we may use your mobile phone number and related information to send you marketing messages, promotional offers, event information, and other communications you have requested.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared with service providers that assist us in delivering our SMS program, such as messaging platforms and telecommunications providers, solely as necessary to provide those services.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Your participation in our SMS program is voluntary. You may opt out of marketing text messages at any time by replying STOP to a message you receive from us. Message and data rates may apply.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies, pixels, analytics technologies, and similar tools to understand how visitors use our website, improve functionality, measure marketing performance, and provide relevant advertising.

Your browser or device may allow you to limit or disable cookies. Doing so may affect the functionality of certain portions of the website.

Payment Information

Ticket purchases and other transactions may be processed through third-party payment and ticketing providers. Payment information you provide during a transaction may be collected and processed directly by those providers in accordance with their own privacy policies and security practices.

We do not necessarily receive or store complete payment card information submitted to third-party payment processors.

How We Share Information

We may share information with vendors and service providers that perform services on our behalf, including ticketing, payment processing, website hosting, analytics, email delivery, SMS delivery, advertising, and customer support.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or governmental request; when necessary to protect our rights, safety, or property or the rights, safety, or property of others; or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, sale, or transfer of all or part of our business.

We do not sell or rent SMS opt-in information or SMS consent to third parties for their marketing purposes.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our website and marketing programs are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information. These may include the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, subject to applicable law.

You may also unsubscribe from email marketing using the unsubscribe link contained in our emails and opt out of SMS marketing by replying STOP to our text messages.

Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we may update the effective date shown at the top of this page. Your continued use of our website and services after an updated policy becomes effective is subject to the revised policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact 13th World Fright Park through the contact information provided on our website.
